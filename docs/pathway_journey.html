Sources and assumptions

Traditional MATS: typical memory assessment service pathway (referral, new patient appointment, brain imaging, MDT, follow-up), from the UK AD CWA CSF Pathway Understanding Report (June 2025, four NHS sites). BHC Phases 0, 1 and 2: the project pathway flow charts (pathway_evolution). Waiting times are deliberately omitted in this version. In the BHC phases, a dementia-stage assessment is drawn as referral back to MATS. Counts show the contacts drawn in the journey that was walked and will differ from first-year totals reported in the source.
